Ayah Chakmakchi is a contemporary artist whose work lives at the intersection of her Spanish and Iraqi heritage. Her paintings, bold silhouettes, flowing shapes, expressive color, carry the warmth and tension of growing up between cultures.

Her path to art wasn't linear. She studied international law and diplomacy in Rome and Paris, but it was living in those cities that pulled her toward creative life. Surrounded by art and the communities that form around it, she discovered something she keeps coming back to: creativity connects people in ways that nothing else does.

Now based in the Bay Area, Ayah is building a space for people of all ages to explore that connection, to slow down, make something, and feel something. Her work invites you to do the same.
